Product decisions
  • Lead with delivery proof and the customer's exchange request. In our merchant interview, all three holds in six months were exchange disputes over courier charges.
  • Never show who complained.
  • Promise a complete, on-time response, not an outcome. Police decide.
  • AI writes only from the case file, tags every line with its proof, and never blames the customer.
  • Rules decide when, AI decides what to say, the merchant decides whether to send.
Left out on purpose
  • Blocks on the merchant's own bank account (v1 exports the case file instead).
  • Large holds that already get a human case manager.
  • Contacting the customer or the police on the merchant's behalf.
  • Refunds and chargebacks.
What we'd measure
  • Share of holds under ₹50,000 answered before the deadline.
  • Time from alert to submitted, median and 90th percentile (goal under 2 min).
  • Money released as a share of money held, and days on hold.
  • Share of officer requests answered within 24 hours.
  • Escalations sent within 1 day of the deadline.
  • Share of Tracer's drafts approved without edits.
  • Guardrail: statements officers flag as inaccurate.
